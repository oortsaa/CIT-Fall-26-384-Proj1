Numbers On The Board: Best Power Forward Candidates

In this episode, the hosts share their picks for the best power forward candidates for the upcoming season.

Premise

The episode goes over the hosts' picks for the top power forwards in the league. Their leading candidates include Jayson Tatum and Giannis Antetokounmpo, with Pascal Siakam and Alex Sarr also discussed. They disagree about the players and ask questions to explain what separates them, including Alex Sarr's potential. Their points are reasonable, and it makes the show more enjoyable when they challenge each other to stick with their reasoning. The episode also covers NBA news, including Jalen Duren's contract negotiations, and ends with Unplugged, a segment where the hosts talk about whatever they want. It is usually one of the funniest and most enjoyable parts of the podcast.

How enjoyable was it?

Overall, this episode was engaging. The hosts' chemistry makes it enjoyable and funny to watch or listen to, and their disagreements keep the discussion interesting.

My rating: 8.7/10. Highly recommended.
